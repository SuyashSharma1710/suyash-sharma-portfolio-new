## Text Animations

- **Text Loop**: Smoothly cycles and rotates through an array of words or phrases in place with customizable transition modes (fade, slide, flip) and timing intervals.
- **Masked Heading**: Uses SVG clipping masks or CSS background-clip to reveal dramatic typographic headings with animated masks and shifting gradient fills.
- **Particle Text**: Converts typography into physics-driven interactive particles on HTML5 Canvas that disperse on mouse hover and spring back into position.
- **Split Flap Text**: Mechanical airport/train schedule display simulation that flips through alphanumeric characters to form words with authentic mechanical flap physics.
- **Warp Text**: Dynamically distorts and bends typographic geometry using SVG displacement filters or Canvas maps reacting to scroll or cursor motion.
- **Stroke Text**: Outlined typographic text animation that traces paths or cycles neon stroke outlines using animated SVG `stroke-dasharray` and `stroke-dashoffset`.
- **Depth Text**: 3D layered typographic effect with perspective shadows, parallax depth, and directional lighting simulation.
- **Fold Text**: 3D origami/paper folding animation where letters or text segments unfold into view along 3D spatial axes.
- **Echo Text**: Generates trailing chromatic, blur, or outline duplicates behind text during motion or cursor interaction, creating an echoing trail effect.
- **Split Text**: Splits headings into individual characters, words, or lines for granular staggered entrance animations (fade, translateY, scale).
- **Blur Text**: Progressive optical blur-to-sharpness text reveal that animates each character or word from Gaussian blur and opacity to crisp focus.
- **Circular Text**: Renders text arranged along a circular path with continuous smooth rotation, radius control, and interactive speed alterations.
- **Text Type**: Typewriter animation that outputs text character by character with blinking caret, typing speed jitter, and backspacing support.
- **Shuffle**: Scrambles characters rapidly with random glyphs and alphanumeric symbols before resolving cleanly into the target phrase.
- **Shiny Text**: Sleek metallic light sweep/shimmer reflection that glides across typography horizontally at regular intervals or on hover.
- **Text Pressure**: Variable typography effect that adjusts font weight, width, and variable font axes dynamically based on mouse proximity and pressure.
- **Curved Loop**: Continuous infinite loop of text scrolling smoothly along custom SVG bezier curves or circular spline paths.
- **Fuzzy Text**: Grainy, noise-textured text shader simulation with glitchy fuzz, chromatic fringe, and TV static distortion.
- **Gradient Text**: Vibrant multi-stop linear or radial animated gradient fills that shift hues across typography continuously.
- **Falling Text**: Gravity and physics-based simulation where individual letter bodies drop, bounce, collide, and settle inside a container.
- **Text Cursor**: Interactive custom cursor trailer that renders floating contextual typography labels or follows pointer coordinates with smooth inertia.
- **Decrypted Text**: Cyberpunk-inspired decryption animation that cycles through hex/binary/matrix characters before locking into final letters.
- **True Focus**: Interactive blur backdrop effect that keeps only the currently hovered word or character sharply in focus while blurring peripheral text.
- **Scroll Float**: Scroll-triggered kinetic text animation where letters float upwards with subtle buoyancy and easing as they enter viewport.
- **Scroll Reveal**: Scroll-driven character-by-character or word-by-word reveal that unveils text based on scroll progress or intersection triggers.
- **ASCII Text**: Renders typography composed of dynamic ASCII character matrices with density shading, dithering, and retro computer styling.
- **Scrambled Text**: Rapidly randomized character scrambling effect that sequentially decodes each letter into place with sound/ticker feel.
- **Rotating Text**: 3D or 2D revolving typography carousel that switches active phrases with smooth flip, cube rotation, or vertical slide.
- **Glitch Text**: Digital artifact and signal interference effect with RGB splitting, displacement slices, and sporadic jitter.
- **Scroll Velocity**: Horizontal infinite marquee ticker whose scroll direction and velocity accelerate dynamically in response to scroll speed.
- **Variable Proximity**: Reactively modifies variable font properties (weight, slant, tracking) in real-time according to cursor distance.
- **Count Up**: Smooth numerical counter with easing functions, decimal formatting, currency/unit prefixes, and intersection observer triggering.
